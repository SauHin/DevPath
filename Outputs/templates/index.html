    02 · How it works

    From a list of tools to a learning order.

    1
    Record your stack

    Search 186 tools or pick from the ones most developers list. One language and two tools in total is enough to get a result.

    2
    Match against 23,387 developers

    Your stack becomes a 186-dimension binary vector, reduced with UMAP on Jaccard distance and assigned to the nearest of 6 K-Means clusters.

    3
    Read the gap

    You get the skills common in your profile that you lack, ranked by how many people in that profile who also lack them say they want to learn them next.
